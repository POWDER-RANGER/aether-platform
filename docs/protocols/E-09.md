# E-09 — Session reliability and stall diagnosis

**Status:** TOOL IMPLEMENTED; owner hardware execution UNVERIFIED  
**Threshold:** **PROPOSED, NOT FROZEN: 30 minutes per session, 10/10 clean sessions.**

## MSI procedure

`python scripts/soak/run_session.py --settings configuration/platform/ENV-01.yaml --minutes 30 --heartbeat-timeout 10 --repeat 10`

The harness launches the companion as a child process, captures stdout/stderr and exit code, monitors the companion main-loop heartbeat, requests faulthandler stack output on a stale heartbeat, then stops the child. It records completed, crashed(exit code), hung, or stalled (from recovery.stall events) under evidence/E-09/. This supports the M1 requirement to diagnose previously observed gameplay stalls and unexpected exits; it does not itself prove the cause. A defect is RESOLVED only after a reproduced cause, evidence-backed diagnosis, fix, and ten subsequent clean runs.
